How it works

  1. 1

    Create a GrepoData account

    An account is required to collect and share enemy city intelligence.

  2. 2

    Join a GrepoData team

    Create a team and invite your allies to share the collected intel.

  3. 3

    Install the userscript

    The userscript adds in-game functionality and links to your GrepoData account.

  4. 4

    Index reports or share commands

    Upload new reports while playing the game by clicking the 'Index +' button on forum and inbox reports.
