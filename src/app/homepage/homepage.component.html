Game statistics

We collect data from the Grepolis API once every hour. You can browse daily scoreboards for each game world as well as detailed player and alliance statistics.

Open source

GrepoData is 100% open source! Interested in contributing? Check out our GitHub page.

Tools

Add our Discord bot to your server or get started using our city indexer userscript to collect & share enemy intelligence with your alliance.
