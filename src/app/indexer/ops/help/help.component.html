Team operations FAQ

How Team Ops works and how to share your commands

What are team operations?

The Team Ops tool allows you to share a command overview with your teammates.

  1. 1

    You can share commands using your in-game command overview (Curator Advisor required).

  2. 2

    Your uploaded commands are shared with the teams that you are currently contributing to.

  3. 3

    On this page, you and your teammates will see a real-time combined command overview.

Share your commands

Userscript required

If you want to start or contribute to an operation, simply upload your commands using the in-game userscript. To do this, you need to have the userscript installed and you need to be part of a team.
