About this scoreboard

This scoreboard shows points gained {{scoreboardDateInfo}}. {{nextUpdate}}.

Data collection delays

Points can take up to 2 hours to show up here. We check every 10 minutes for new data, but Grepolis publishes it with a delay that we cannot influence, and sometimes the published data is older than the previous update.

Daily reset

The scoreboard resets at 02:00 every night rather than at midnight, because of the data processing delay. Few points are gained between 00:00 and 02:00, so the risk of counting too many is low. The totals are never 100% accurate.

Published siege reports

Team admins of the City Indexer can publish an indexed siege here, and unpublish it again. Nothing is made public without their opt-in: everyone sees the siege overview, while the individual attacks stay visible to the team only.

World map

An island counts towards an alliance if that alliance owns the most towns on it, with or without farm villages. It approximates who controls what; the percentages are not the domination end-game percentages.
